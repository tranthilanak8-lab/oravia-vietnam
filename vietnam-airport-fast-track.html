Frequently Asked Questions

Which airports in Vietnam do you cover?

Our main Fast Track services are at Noi Bai International Airport (HAN) in Hanoi and Tan Son Nhat International Airport (SGN) in Ho Chi Minh City. Other airports can be arranged on request.

Does Fast Track replace the need for a visa?

No. Fast Track speeds up the airport process, but you still need a valid visa or visa exemption to enter Vietnam.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 48 to 72 hours before your flight. Last-minute requests may be possible depending on availability.
