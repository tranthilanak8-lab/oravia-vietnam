Multi-day Tours · North Vietnam · Sapa

Sapa 2 Days / 1 Night – Cat Cat & Fansipan

Hotel stay in Sapa town, with the Fansipan cable car included

An easy-paced Sapa break with a hotel in town. Walk down to Cat Cat village and its waterfall on day one, then ride the cable car up Fansipan, the highest peak in Indochina, on day two before free time at Sapa market.

Duration2 days / 1 night
RouteHanoi · Sapa · Cat Cat · Fansipan
Meals2 lunches · 1 dinner · 1 breakfast
DepartureDaily

Itinerary

Day 1 · Hanoi – Sapa – Cat Cat Village (L/D)

  1. 06:30 – 06:45

    Pick-up at the meeting point by sleeper bus. About 5 hours on the expressway to Sapa.

  2. 12:30 – 13:00

    Check in at your hotel and have lunch.

  3. 14:00

    Walk with your guide to Cat Cat, a H'mong village where you learn about local customs and crafts. Visit Cat Cat waterfall and the hydroelectric station built by the French in the early 20th century.

  4. 19:00

    Dinner at a restaurant. Overnight at a hotel in Sapa town.

Day 2 · Sapa – Fansipan – Hanoi (B/L)

  1. 07:30

    Breakfast, then ride the Fansipan cable car to the summit of the "Roof of Indochina" (3,143 m). Alternatively, spend the morning exploring Love Waterfall or the Muong Hoa Valley on your own.

  2. 12:00

    Lunch and hotel check-out. Free time to browse Sapa market.

  3. 15:30

    Board the bus back to Hanoi.

  4. 21:00

    Arrive in Hanoi. Tour ends.

Included

  • Sleeper or VIP cabin bus Hanoi – Sapa – Hanoi
  • 1 night at a hotel in Sapa town (2–3 guests per room)
  • Meals as listed: 3 main meals and 1 snack
  • English-speaking guide
  • Fansipan cable car ticket
  • Sightseeing entrance fees as listed

Not included

  • Drinks with meals
  • Travel insurance
  • Tips and personal expenses

Good to know

  • Fansipan summit can be cold and cloudy even in summer; bring a warm jacket.
  • Children aged 1–4 travel free (one free child per two adults, no bus seat). Ages 5–9 pay 75% of the adult price; ages 10 and over pay the adult price.
  • Rates on public holidays may differ; please ask when you enquire.
