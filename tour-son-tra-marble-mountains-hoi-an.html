Son Tra – Marble Mountains – Hoi An

Afternoon and evening with dinner

An afternoon-to-evening tour: Lady Buddha at Linh Ung Pagoda on the Son Tra peninsula, the caves and pagodas of the Marble Mountains, then Hoi An ancient town lit by thousands of lanterns.

DurationHalf day + evening (about 7 hours)
RouteDa Nang · Son Tra · Marble Mountains · Hoi An
MealsDinner
Pick-upDa Nang hotels

Itinerary

  1. 14:00 – 14:30

    Pick-up at your hotel in Da Nang. Drive along the coast road to the Son Tra peninsula and visit Linh Ung Pagoda, a symbol of peace for the city, with sweeping views from the top.

  2. 16:00

    Visit the Marble Mountains: natural caves, hidden pagodas and the stone-carving village.

  3. 17:30

    Head to Hoi An to see the Fujian Assembly Hall and the Japanese Covered Bridge.

  4. 18:30

    Free time to stroll and shop under the lanterns.

  5. 19:00

    Dinner at a local restaurant.

  6. 20:00 – 20:30

    Drive back to Da Nang.

  7. 21:00

    Drop-off at your hotel. Tour ends.

Included

  • Transport
  • English-speaking tour guide
  • Entrance fees
  • Dinner
  • Bottled water

Not included

  • Drinks
  • Tips for guide and driver
  • Personal expenses

Good to know

  • Children under 5 travel free; ages 5–9 pay 50% of the adult price; ages 10 and over pay the adult price.
  • Pick-up from Hoi An hotels can be arranged on request.
  • The order of the itinerary may change on the day.
