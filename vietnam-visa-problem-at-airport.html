Visa Problem at the Airport Right Now?

Already at check-in or the gate and your Vietnam eVisa won't go through? Here's why it happens.

It is one of the worst moments a traveller can face: at the check-in counter or the immigration desk, staff say your Vietnam eVisa cannot be used, and boarding is refused. Usually the cause is simple but easy to miss until the very last minute: a detail on the eVisa that does not match the passport, an entry date or airport that does not line up with the actual flight, or a visa that has already expired or been used.

Airlines are required to check that a passenger holds a valid, matching entry document before boarding, so once this is spotted at the gate, it is almost always too late to fix on the spot. The real fix happens earlier: checking every detail on an approved eVisa against the passport as soon as it is issued, not on travel day.

Why this happens

  • The name, passport number or date of birth on the eVisa does not exactly match the passport
  • The entry or exit airport/border gate does not match where the traveller is flying from or to
  • The eVisa's validity period does not cover the actual travel date
  • A single-entry eVisa was already used on an earlier entry
  • The application was only submitted, never actually approved, and the traveller assumed it was ready

If your trip is still a few days away and you have not applied yet, see Urgent Vietnam Visa Assistance. If you already applied and it is still processing, see Expedite Vietnam eVisa. If you suspect an error in your application before you travel, see Fix eVisa Service.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why did my approved eVisa get rejected at check-in?

The most common reasons are a mismatch between the eVisa and passport details, an incorrect entry or exit point, or travelling outside the eVisa's valid dates.

Can the airline let me board anyway?

That decision belongs entirely to the airline. Airlines are responsible for checking valid entry documents before boarding and can refuse boarding if a visa does not match requirements.

Can a new eVisa be issued instantly at the airport?

No. Approval depends on the Vietnamese immigration authorities and normal processing time, not on any travel agency.
