VEHICLE PHOTOS
The vehicle photographs in assets/vehicles/ were supplied by the website owner in this project and are used as provided.

CRUISE VISUALS
The cruise cards use original ORAVIA SVG representative illustrations created for this website. They are intentionally not photographs of the named vessels. This avoids using a third-party ship photograph without permission.

Cruise catalogue names are product/operator names used for customer enquiry and comparison. Final vessel, cabin, itinerary and availability must be confirmed before booking.
