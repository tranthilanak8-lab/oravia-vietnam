Saigon by Night: Water Puppets & Dinner Cruise

Evening · 18:00 – 21:30

An elegant evening in Saigon: a water puppet show with live traditional music, then dinner on a cruise along the illuminated Saigon River past Ba Son Bridge, Thu Thiem and Landmark 81.

DurationEvening (about 3.5 hours)
RouteHo Chi Minh City
MealsDinner
Pick-upDistrict 1 hotels or a central meeting point

Itinerary

  1. 18:00

    Pick-up in District 1.

  2. 18:30

    Water puppet show with a live traditional orchestra.

  3. 19:30

    Board the dinner cruise: Vietnamese set menu or buffet, live music and night views along the river.

  4. 21:30

    Return to your hotel.

Included

  • Air-conditioned minivan
  • English-speaking guide
  • Water puppet show ticket
  • Dinner cruise (set menu or buffet)
  • Taxes and service charges

Not included

  • Drinks on board
  • Tips and personal expenses
  • Pick-up outside District 1

Good to know

  • Children under 3 travel free (one child per two adults); ages 3–9 pay 75% of the adult price; ages 10 and over pay the adult price.
  • Timings are approximate and may change with traffic and weather.
