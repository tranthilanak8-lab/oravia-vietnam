Urgent Vietnam Visa Assistance

Flying soon and still waiting for your visa? Talk to a team that handles urgent cases every day.

100% Guaranteed

It happens more often than you think. Your flight is in a few days, or even tomorrow, and you realise you still need a Vietnam visa. Or you applied weeks ago and the status has not changed. Most airlines will not let you board without a valid visa or proof of visa exemption, so time matters.

Oravia's Urgent Visa Assistance is for travellers in exactly this situation. We look at your case quickly, explain the real options and help you move forward, without false promises.

When should you ask for urgent help?

  • Your flight to Vietnam leaves in the next few days and you have not applied yet
  • Your application was sent back for correction and you are running out of time
  • Your plans changed and you need to arrive earlier than the date on your eVisa
  • You are already travelling in the region and decided to visit Vietnam at short notice

What we can and cannot promise

We will always be honest with you. Processing times depend on the Vietnamese immigration authorities, your nationality, the day of the week and public holidays. If we believe your timeframe is not realistic, we will tell you straight away so you can change your plans in time.

Urgent visa tips

  • Do not submit several applications for the same trip. Duplicate applications can slow things down.
  • Check your airline's boarding rules. Many ask to see your eVisa or visa-exemption status at check-in.
  • Make sure the entry date on your application is not later than your actual arrival date.
  • If your trip falls over a weekend or public holiday, contact us as early as possible.

Already applied and your eVisa is still processing? See Expedite Vietnam eVisa. Travelling over a weekend or a Vietnamese public holiday? See Vietnam Visa at Weekends & Holidays. Want a smoother arrival too? Add Arrival Fast Track to skip the immigration queue.

Need your visa urgently?

Send your nationality, flight date and arrival airport. The sooner we hear from you, the more options you have.

Oravia is a private travel and visa-assistance company, not a government agency. Visa decisions are made by the competent Vietnamese authorities. Rules can change, so always check the latest information on the official Vietnam eVisa portal before you travel.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get a Vietnam visa in one or two days?

In some cases, faster processing is possible, but it depends on your nationality, the day of the week and the authorities' workload. Send us your details and we will tell you what is realistic for your case.

My eVisa is still processing and I fly soon. What should I do?

Do not submit a new application straight away. Send us your application code and flight details. We will check the situation and advise on the best next step.

Will I be refunded if my visa is not ready in time?

We confirm the service, timeframe and conditions before you pay, so you know exactly what is included.

Can you help on a Saturday or Sunday?

Yes. See our Visa at Weekends & Holidays service. Support outside normal working days depends on team and processing availability.
