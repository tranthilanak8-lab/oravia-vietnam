Vietnam Domestic Fast Track

Flying within Vietnam, or connecting from an international flight? Let us guide you.

Domestic flights are the fastest way to travel between Hanoi, Da Nang, Ho Chi Minh City, Phu Quoc and Nha Trang. But domestic terminals can be busy, signs are not always clear, and connecting from an international flight often means changing terminals. Domestic Fast Track gives you a local assistant to handle it with you.

What's included

  • Meet & greet at the terminal entrance or arrival gate
  • Assistance with check-in and baggage drop
  • Escort through security, using priority lanes where available
  • Guidance to your gate, or to baggage claim and the exit on arrival

International-to-domestic connections

Many travellers arrive in Vietnam on an international flight and continue straight on a domestic flight. At the main airports this usually means clearing immigration and customs, collecting your luggage and moving to a different terminal:

  • Noi Bai (HAN), Hanoi: international flights use Terminal 2. Most domestic flights use Terminal 1.
  • Tan Son Nhat (SGN), Ho Chi Minh City: international flights use Terminal 2. Domestic flights use Terminal 1 or Terminal 3, depending on the airline.

Combine Arrival Fast Track with Domestic Fast Track and an Oravia assistant will guide you through immigration, baggage and the terminal change to your next check-in.

Who is it for?

  • Travellers with a tight international-to-domestic connection
  • Families, older travellers and passengers who need extra help
  • Groups and tour participants travelling together
  • Anyone who wants a calm, guided airport experience

What to send us

  • Your domestic flight number, date and time
  • Your international arrival flight, if you are connecting
  • Number of passengers and pieces of luggage

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need Fast Track for a domestic flight?

It is not required, but it is very helpful if you have a tight connection, are travelling with family or older travellers, or simply want support at a busy airport.

Do I need to change terminals when connecting in Hanoi or Ho Chi Minh City?

Usually yes. International and domestic flights use different terminals at both Noi Bai and Tan Son Nhat. Our assistant guides you between them.

Which airports are covered?

Our main Domestic Fast Track service is at Noi Bai (HAN) and Tan Son Nhat (SGN). Other airports can be arranged on request.
