Multi-day Tours · North Vietnam · Sapa

Sapa 2 Days / 1 Night – Village Homestay

Overnight in a local homestay in Ta Van village

A short, active escape to the Sapa highlands. Trek through the Muong Hoa Valley to H'mong and Dao villages, learn to cook spring rolls with a local family and spend the night in a village homestay.

Duration2 days / 1 night
RouteHanoi · Sapa · Muong Hoa Valley
Meals2 lunches · 1 dinner · 1 breakfast
Pick-upHanoi Old Quarter

Itinerary

Day 1 · Hanoi – Sapa – Y Linh Ho – Lao Chai – Ta Van (L/D)

  1. 06:00 – 06:15

    Pick-up at your hotel in Hanoi's Old Quarter and board the sleeper bus to Sapa via the Noi Bai – Lao Cai expressway. Enjoy mountain views and terraced fields along the way, with a 30-minute rest stop.

  2. 12:30 – 13:00

    Arrive in Sapa and have lunch.

  3. 14:00

    Start the afternoon trek: about 6 km along the slopes of the Muong Hoa Valley to Y Linh Ho, a Black H'mong village, then about 3 km more to Lao Chai, crossing a small bridge into the valley.

  4. 16:00

    Reach Ta Van, a village around 8 km from Sapa town, home to Giay and H'mong communities and known for its ancient carved rocks and stilt houses.

  5. 17:00

    Check in at the homestay. Join an optional cooking class and learn to make Vietnamese spring rolls.

  6. 18:30

    Dinner with local dishes. Overnight at the homestay.

Day 2 · Ta Van – Giang Ta Chai – Su Pan – Sapa – Hanoi (B/L)

  1. 07:00 – 08:00

    Breakfast at the homestay.

  2. Morning

    Trek about 8 km to Giang Ta Chai and Su Pan, two Red Dao villages known for their colourful dress and traditional customs.

  3. 12:00

    Lunch and a rest at the homestay.

  4. 14:00

    Transfer by car back to Sapa town.

  5. 15:00 – 15:30

    Board the bus back to Hanoi.

  6. 21:00 – 21:30

    Arrive in Hanoi. Tour ends.

Included

  • Air-conditioned sleeper bus Hanoi – Sapa – Hanoi (Wi-Fi, blanket and pillow)
  • 1 night in a homestay in Ta Van village
  • English-speaking local guide in Sapa
  • Meals as listed in the itinerary
  • Entrance fees

Not included

  • Drinks
  • Travel insurance
  • Tips for guide and driver
  • Personal expenses

Good to know

  • The order of activities may change depending on weather and trail conditions.
  • Treks are moderate; comfortable walking shoes and a light rain jacket are recommended.
  • Children under 5 travel free when sharing a bed with parents; ages 5–8 pay 75% of the adult price; ages 9 and over pay the adult price.
