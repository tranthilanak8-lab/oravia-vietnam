Saigon Classic City Tour by Motorbike

Half day · 08:00 or 13:00

Ride pillion with a local driver to Saigon's must-sees: the War Remnants Museum, Notre Dame Cathedral, the Central Post Office, the Opera House, City Hall, Ben Thanh Market and Nguyen Hue walking street.

Duration3.5 – 4 hours
RouteHo Chi Minh City
MealsA drink
Pick-upDistrict 1 hotels or a central meeting point

Itinerary

  1. 08:00 or 13:00

    Pick-up at your hotel.

  2. On the tour

    War Remnants Museum; Notre Dame Cathedral, built 1863–1880; the Central Post Office; the Opera House and City Hall; Ben Thanh Market; Nguyen Hue walking street.

  3. 3.5 – 4 hours later

    Drop-off at your hotel.

Included

  • English-speaking guide and licensed driver
  • Motorbike, fuel and quality helmet
  • Rain poncho if needed
  • Hotel pick-up and drop-off in Districts 1, 3 and 4
  • All entrance tickets
  • A drink

Not included

  • Lunch
  • Personal expenses
  • VAT

Good to know

  • Children under 3 travel free (one child per two adults); ages 3–9 pay 75% of the adult price; ages 10 and over pay the adult price.
  • Suitable for children.
  • Timings are approximate and may change with traffic and weather.
