Vietnam Departure Fast Track

End your trip the way it started: relaxed, guided and on time.

Leaving Vietnam means check-in, security screening and exit immigration, often with long queues at busy airports. If you are short on time, travelling with family or simply want a calm end to your trip, Departure Fast Track makes the process easier.

What's included

  • Meet at the terminal entrance with your name on a sign
  • Check-in assistance with your airline
  • Escort through security, using priority lanes where available
  • Guidance through exit immigration, using priority lanes where available
  • Directions to your gate or lounge

When Departure Fast Track makes sense

  • Early-morning or late-evening flights, when queues can build quickly
  • Travelling with young children, older family members or lots of luggage
  • Tight schedules after a last meeting or a late hotel check-out
  • Groups who want to check in and move through the airport together

Airports

Departure Fast Track is available at Noi Bai International Airport (HAN) and Tan Son Nhat International Airport (SGN). Other airports can be arranged on request.

Departure tips

  • Arrive at the airport at least 3 hours before an international flight
  • Keep your passport and eVisa (or entry stamp) easy to reach
  • Check that you are leaving Vietnam within the permitted stay shown on your visa or entry stamp
  • Book a private transfer to the airport so you arrive on time and relaxed

Frequently Asked Questions

How early should I arrive for Departure Fast Track?

We recommend arriving about 3 hours before an international flight. Fast Track helps you move through the airport, but airline check-in deadlines still apply.

Is Departure Fast Track available for domestic flights?

Yes. See our Domestic Fast Track service for domestic flights within Vietnam.

Can you meet me at the terminal entrance?

Yes. Your confirmation includes a clear meeting point at the terminal entrance and a contact number.
