Tan Son Nhat Airport Fast Track (SGN), Ho Chi Minh City

Vietnam's busiest airport, made simple. Meet your assistant and move through immigration faster.

Tan Son Nhat International Airport (SGN) is Vietnam's busiest airport and the main gateway to Ho Chi Minh City, southern Vietnam and the Mekong Delta. International flights use the international terminal (Terminal 2). Domestic flights use Terminal 1 or the newer Terminal 3, depending on the airline.

With a large number of flights, immigration queues at SGN can be long, especially at peak times. Oravia's Tan Son Nhat Fast Track gives you a dedicated assistant and a faster route through arrival formalities.

What's included at Tan Son Nhat

  • Meet & greet in the international arrival area, with your name on a sign
  • Escort to the priority immigration lane, where available
  • Assistance with arrival procedures
  • Guidance to baggage claim and customs
  • Escort to the arrival hall to meet your driver

After Fast Track: getting into the city

Tan Son Nhat is inside the city, about 7 to 8 km from District 1. The trip usually takes 30 to 60 minutes, depending on traffic. Book a private airport transfer and skip the taxi queue.

Travelling on to Vung Tau or the Mekong Delta? A private car with driver can take you there directly from the airport.

Connecting to a domestic flight in Ho Chi Minh City?

If you arrive on an international flight and continue on a domestic flight, you will need to move from the international terminal to the correct domestic terminal. Our Domestic Fast Track helps you make that connection smoothly.

What to send us

  • Flight number and arrival date and time
  • Number of passengers
  • Your visa status: eVisa, visa exemption or other
  • Whether you also need a transfer or a domestic connection

Frequently Asked Questions

Which terminal do international flights use at Tan Son Nhat?

International flights use the international terminal (Terminal 2). Domestic flights use Terminal 1 or Terminal 3, depending on the airline.

How far is Tan Son Nhat Airport from District 1?

About 7 to 8 km. Travel time is usually 30 to 60 minutes, depending on traffic.

Is Fast Track worth it at Tan Son Nhat?

SGN is Vietnam's busiest airport, and queues can be long at peak times. Fast Track is especially useful for families, older travellers, business travellers and anyone arriving at busy hours.
