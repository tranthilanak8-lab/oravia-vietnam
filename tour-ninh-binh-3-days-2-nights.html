Multi-day Tours · North Vietnam · Ninh Binh

Ninh Binh 3 Days / 2 Nights

Stay in a bungalow or 3–4★ hotel

Take your time in Ninh Binh: Hoa Lu, Tam Coc and Mua Cave, a day at Thung Nham Bird Valley or Cuc Phuong National Park, and Bai Dinh with Trang An to finish.

Duration3 days / 2 nights
RouteHanoi · Ninh Binh
MealsVietnamese meals as listed
Pick-upHanoi Old Quarter hotels

Itinerary

Day 1 · Hoa Lu – Tam Coc – Mua Cave

  1. 07:25 – 08:00

    Pick-up from your hotel in Hanoi's Old Quarter or the Opera House area, then depart for Ninh Binh. Exact pick-up time depends on your hotel location.

  2. 09:15

    Short 15–20 minute rest stop on the way.

  3. 10:15

    Visit Hoa Lu, Vietnam's ancient capital from 968 to 1010, and learn about the Dinh, Le and Ly dynasties. Then cycle through the surrounding village to see local life up close.

  4. 12:00

    Buffet lunch at a spacious local restaurant with Ninh Binh specialities — goat meat, spring rolls, fish, chicken, fried rice and fresh salads. Vegetarian dishes are always available.

  5. 13:30

    A 1.5-hour bamboo boat ride through Tam Coc, gliding past rice paddies, limestone peaks and river caves — the landscape locals call “Ha Long Bay on land”.

  6. 15:30

    Continue to Mua Cave (Dragon Peak). Climb almost 500 steps to the top of Lying Dragon Mountain for a sweeping panoramic view over Tam Coc and, in summer, the lotus fields below.

  7. 17:00

    Check in to your bungalow or hotel and relax (pool available in summer at many properties).

  8. 19:00

    Dinner, then an evening stroll around Tam Coc. Overnight in Ninh Binh.

Day 2 · Option 1: Bich Dong – Thai Vi – Thung Nham Bird Valley

  1. Morning

    Breakfast at your hotel.

  2. 09:00

    Cycle with your guide to Thai Vi Temple and Bich Dong Pagoda, often called the second most beautiful cave in Vietnam.

  3. 11:30

    Check out and enjoy lunch with local specialities. Vegetarian options available.

  4. 14:00

    Thung Nham Bird Valley: take a small sampan into Hang But (Buddha Cave), about 500 m long, with a Buddha-shaped stalactite beside an underground river, then visit a tropical fruit orchard. In the late afternoon, paddle quietly through the valley as thousands of birds from around 40 species return to roost.

  5. 16:00

    Back to your bungalow or hotel to relax.

  6. 19:00

    Dinner, then an evening stroll around Tam Coc. Overnight in Ninh Binh.

Day 2 · Option 2: Cuc Phuong National Park

  1. Morning

    Breakfast, then set off for Cuc Phuong National Park, Vietnam's first national park (founded in 1962), about 100 km south-west of Hanoi.

  2. 10:30

    Endangered Primate Rescue Center, a non-profit that rescues, breeds and protects endangered primates.

  3. 12:00

    Lunch with local dishes, then a short trek into the old-growth forest and a visit to the Cave of Prehistoric Man.

  4. 16:00

    Return to your accommodation in Ninh Binh. Overnight in Ninh Binh.

Day 3 · Bai Dinh – Trang An

  1. Morning

    Breakfast, then check out.

  2. 09:30

    Transfer to Bai Dinh Pagoda.

  3. 10:30

    Visit Bai Dinh, one of the largest Buddhist complexes in Southeast Asia, home to 500 stone Arhat statues, a 36-tonne bronze bell and a 100-tonne bronze Buddha.

  4. 12:30

    Buffet lunch at a spacious local restaurant with Ninh Binh specialities — goat meat, spring rolls, fish, chicken, fried rice and fresh salads. Vegetarian dishes are always available.

  5. 13:30

    A 2-hour boat journey through Trang An, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2014, known for its remarkable network of water caves and tunnels.

  6. 16:00

    Return to Hanoi.

  7. 18:30

    Drop-off at your hotel. Tour ends.

Included

  • Limousine van (17–22 seats)
  • English-speaking guide
  • Entrance fees
  • Electric car at Bai Dinh (both ways)
  • Bicycle for the village ride
  • Vietnamese meals as listed
  • 2 nights in a bungalow or 3–4★ hotel
  • Conical hat or raincoat to borrow, depending on the weather

Not included

  • Drinks
  • Travel insurance
  • Tips for guide and driver
  • Personal expenses

Good to know

  • The order of the itinerary may change depending on the weather or your guide's arrangements.
  • Single rooms are available on request.
