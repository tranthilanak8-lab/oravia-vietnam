Multi-day Tours · South Vietnam

Mekong Delta & Phu Quoc 3 Days / 2 Nights

Cai Rang floating market · Rach Gia · boat to Phu Quoc

Cross the Mekong Delta on your way to the island: My Tho's pagoda, river islands and canals on day one, Can Tho's Cai Rang floating market on day two, a night in Rach Gia, then the morning boat to Phu Quoc.

Duration3 days / 2 nights
RouteHo Chi Minh City · My Tho · Can Tho · Rach Gia · Phu Quoc
Meals2 breakfasts · 2 lunches
DepartureDaily · join-in group

Itinerary

Day 1 · Ho Chi Minh City – My Tho – Can Tho (L)

  1. 07:30

    Pick-up in Ho Chi Minh City and drive into the delta, with a comfort stop on the way.

  2. 09:30

    Visit Vinh Trang Pagoda.

  3. 10:00

    Boat from My Tho to Unicorn Island: coconut candy and coconut wine, tropical fruit with southern folk music, a rowboat through a small canal and honey tea at a bee farm.

  4. 11:30

    Ride a motorised rickshaw through the village.

  5. 12:00

    Lunch at a local restaurant.

  6. 14:00

    Drive to Can Tho and check in around 17:00. Free evening to explore the city.

Day 2 · Cai Rang Floating Market – Rach Gia (B/L)

  1. 06:30

    Morning boat to Cai Rang Floating Market, then walk through the village and orchards and visit a rice-husking mill and a rice-noodle workshop.

  2. Late morning

    Check out and have lunch at a local restaurant.

  3. 13:30

    Drive to Rach Gia. Overnight in Rach Gia.

Day 3 · Rach Gia – Phu Quoc Island (B)

  1. 06:30

    Breakfast at the hotel.

  2. 07:30

    Transfer to the ferry terminal for check-in.

  3. 08:00

    Boat to Phu Quoc, arriving around 12:00. Tour ends on the island.

Included

  • Transport throughout
  • English-speaking guide
  • Boat ticket Rach Gia – Phu Quoc
  • Meals as listed
  • Boat trips, rowboat, motorised rickshaw and bike riding
  • Fruit, honey tea and coconut candy tasting
  • Entrance fees
  • 2 nights in hotels (air-conditioned double or twin room)
  • Bottled water

Not included

  • Travel insurance
  • Single room supplement
  • Tips and personal expenses

Good to know

  • The tour ends in Phu Quoc; we can arrange your island hotel, tours and return flight on request.
  • Children under 3 travel free (one child per two adults); ages 3–9 pay 75% of the adult price; ages 10 and over pay the adult price.
  • The schedule may change with traffic and weather.
