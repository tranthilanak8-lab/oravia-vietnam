Vietnam Visa at Weekends & Public Holidays

When government offices close, your travel plans don't have to stop.

Vietnam's immigration offices mainly process visa applications on working days. That means applications submitted on a Friday evening, over a weekend or during a public holiday can wait several days before anyone looks at them. For travellers with a close departure date, this can be stressful.

Oravia's Visa at Weekends & Holidays service is for urgent situations outside normal working days, subject to team and processing availability.

Vietnamese public holidays that affect visa processing

  • New Year's Day, 1 January
  • Tet (Lunar New Year), the longest holiday of the year, usually in late January or February
  • Hung Kings Commemoration Day, 10th day of the 3rd lunar month, usually in April
  • Reunification Day and International Labour Day, 30 April and 1 May
  • National Day, 2 September, often combined with an extra day off

Around Tet especially, government offices can be closed for a week or more and demand for visas is very high. If you plan to travel to Vietnam around Tet, apply several weeks in advance.

Plan ahead: our advice

  • Check the Vietnamese public holiday calendar before you book your flights
  • Apply at least two to three weeks before travel during holiday periods
  • Avoid submitting on a Friday afternoon if your flight is early the next week
  • Keep your passport, photo and travel details ready so nothing is delayed on your side

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Vietnam eVisa processed at weekends?

Visa processing mainly happens on working days. Weekends and public holidays are generally not counted in the standard processing time.

How does Tet affect Vietnam visa applications?

Tet is Vietnam's biggest holiday. Government offices can be closed for about a week, and many travellers apply at the same time. Apply several weeks before your trip if you travel around Tet.

Can Oravia get my visa during a public holiday?

We support urgent cases outside working days, but results depend on processing availability. We will explain what is realistic before you confirm the service.
