Bay Mau Coconut Forest & Hoi An by Night

Afternoon and evening with dinner

Paddle a round basket boat through the Bay Mau water-coconut forest, try crab catching and coconut-leaf crafts, then wander Hoi An ancient town and its night market.

DurationHalf day + evening (about 5.5 hours)
RouteDa Nang · Cam Thanh · Hoi An
MealsDinner
Pick-upDa Nang hotels

Itinerary

  1. 15:30

    Pick-up at your hotel in Da Nang and drive to Cam Thanh, Hoi An.

  2. 16:00

    Explore the coconut forest by basket boat: catch crabs and snails, make toys from coconut leaves and join a basket boat race.

  3. 17:30

    Visit Hoi An ancient town: the Japanese Covered Bridge, the Sa Huynh Culture Museum and an old merchant house, then walk the lantern streets and Nguyen Hoang night market.

  4. 19:00

    Dinner at a local restaurant.

  5. 20:00 – 20:30

    Drive back to Da Nang.

  6. 21:00

    Drop-off at your hotel. Tour ends.

Included

  • Transport
  • English-speaking tour guide
  • Entrance fees
  • Dinner
  • Bottled water

Not included

  • Drinks
  • Tips for guide and driver
  • Personal expenses

Good to know

  • Children under 5 travel free; ages 5–9 pay 50% of the adult price; ages 10 and over pay the adult price.
  • Pick-up from Hoi An hotels can be arranged on request.
  • The order of the itinerary may change on the day.
