Vietnam eVisa Assistance for Foreign Travellers

Get your Vietnam eVisa right the first time, with a local team checking every detail before you submit.

Vietnam's electronic visa has made travel to the country much easier. Since 15 August 2023, citizens of all countries and territories can apply for a Vietnam eVisa online, with a stay of up to 90 days and a choice of single or multiple entry.

The process looks simple. In practice, a small mistake can cause a delay, a request for correction or a problem at check-in. Examples include a name in the wrong order, one wrong passport digit, an unsuitable photo or the wrong entry airport. That is where Oravia helps.

Vietnam eVisa at a glance

  • Validity: up to 90 days
  • Entry type: single entry or multiple entry
  • Who can apply: citizens of all countries and territories
  • How to apply: online, with payment and result delivered electronically
  • Entry: through the international airports, land borders and seaports designated for eVisa holders

Do you need a visa for Vietnam?

It depends on your nationality, the length of your stay and your travel plans. Citizens of some countries can enter Vietnam visa-free for a set number of days. If your nationality is not exempt, if you plan to stay longer than the exemption allows, or if you want to leave and re-enter Vietnam during your trip, you will usually need a visa. The eVisa is the most common option.

Not sure? Send us your nationality and travel dates and we will tell you what applies to your trip. Travelling on a passport from Sri Lanka, Israel, Mauritius, Egypt or another country where applications need extra care? See eVisa for Special Nationalities.

Common eVisa mistakes we help you avoid

  • Full name not matching the passport exactly, including middle names and name order
  • Typos in passport number, date of birth or passport expiry date
  • Portrait photo that is blurred, too dark, cropped or taken with glasses or a hat
  • Unclear or cut-off image of the passport data page
  • Choosing single entry when your itinerary needs multiple entry, for example a side trip to Cambodia or Laos
  • Entry date or entry port that does not match your actual flight
  • Passport with less than six months' validity remaining

Any of these can lead to a correction request, a refusal or an eVisa that does not match your passport. A mismatch can stop you from boarding your flight.

What to prepare

  • Passport valid for at least six months from your entry date, with blank pages
  • A clear image of your passport data page
  • A recent portrait photo with a plain background, looking straight at the camera, without glasses
  • Your planned entry and exit dates, and the airport or border crossing you will use
  • Your first address in Vietnam, such as your hotel

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a Vietnam eVisa take?

Standard processing is usually around three working days, but it can take longer at busy times or if the application needs correction. Weekends and public holidays are usually not counted. Apply at least one to two weeks before your trip whenever possible.

How long can I stay in Vietnam with an eVisa?

A Vietnam eVisa can be issued for up to 90 days, with single or multiple entry. Your permitted stay follows the dates shown on your eVisa.

Single entry or multiple entry: which do I need?

Choose multiple entry if you plan to leave Vietnam and come back during the same trip, for example a side trip to Cambodia, Laos or Thailand. If you enter once and leave once, single entry is enough.

Can Oravia guarantee my eVisa approval?

No. Visa decisions are made by the Vietnamese immigration authorities. We help you submit a complete and accurate application, which reduces the risk of avoidable problems.

Is Oravia the official Vietnam eVisa website?

No. Oravia is a private assistance service. The official government portal is evisa.gov.vn. You can apply there yourself, or use Oravia if you would like expert help and support.
