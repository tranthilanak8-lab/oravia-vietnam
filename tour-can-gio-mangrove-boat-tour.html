Can Gio Mangrove & Wildlife by Speedboat

Full day with lunch · small group (max 12)

Speed down the river to Can Gio, Vietnam's UNESCO mangrove biosphere reserve: row through the mangroves, meet macaques, visit a crocodile reserve and climb a 26 m tower for views over the forest.

DurationFull day (about 7 hours)
RouteHo Chi Minh City · Can Gio
MealsLunch
Pick-upDistrict 1 hotels or a central meeting point

Itinerary

  1. 08:00 – 08:30

    Pick-up in District 1 and depart by speedboat.

  2. 09:30

    Gentle rowing-boat trip through the shaded mangroves near the bat sanctuary.

  3. 10:00

    Vam Sat eco-zone: the crocodile reserve, forest trails to the bird sanctuary (seasonal), long-tailed macaques and the Tang Bong lookout tower.

  4. 12:30

    Lunch with Can Gio specialities.

  5. 13:30

    Return by speedboat, arriving around 15:00–15:30.

Included

  • Return speedboat
  • English-speaking guide
  • Entrance fees
  • Rowing boat in the mangroves
  • Lunch
  • Bottled water

Not included

  • Travel insurance
  • Drinks and meals not listed
  • Tips and personal expenses

Good to know

  • Children under 3 travel free (one child per two adults); ages 3–9 pay 75% of the adult price; ages 10 and over pay the adult price.
  • Timings are approximate and may change with traffic and weather.
