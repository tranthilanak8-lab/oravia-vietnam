Hue City Tour from Da Nang

Full day with lunch

A day in the old imperial capital: the Hai Van tunnel and Lang Co Beach on the way, the tomb of Emperor Khai Dinh, the Imperial City of the Nguyen kings and Thien Mu Pagoda.

DurationFull day (about 10 hours)
RouteDa Nang · Lang Co · Hue
MealsLunch
Pick-upDa Nang hotels

Itinerary

  1. 07:30 – 08:15

    Pick-up at your hotel in Da Nang and drive to Hue through the Hai Van road tunnel, the longest in Southeast Asia, passing Lang Co Beach.

  2. 11:00

    Visit the tomb of Emperor Khai Dinh, a striking blend of Eastern and Western architecture.

  3. 12:00

    Lunch and a rest at a local restaurant.

  4. 13:00

    Explore the Imperial City, palace of the 13 Nguyen kings: Ngo Mon Gate, the Royal Library, Thai Hoa Palace and the Nine Dynastic Urns.

  5. 14:45

    Visit Thien Mu Pagoda on the Perfume River, closely tied to Hue's history and culture.

  6. 15:30

    Drive back to Da Nang.

  7. 17:15 – 17:45

    Drop-off at your hotel. Tour ends.

Included

  • Transport
  • English-speaking tour guide
  • Entrance fees
  • Lunch
  • Bottled water

Not included

  • Drinks
  • Tips for guide and driver
  • Personal expenses

Good to know

  • Children under 5 travel free; ages 5–9 pay 50% of the adult price; ages 10 and over pay the adult price.
  • Pick-up from Hoi An hotels can be arranged on request.
  • The order of the itinerary may change on the day.
