Official eVisa information

What the official portal says.

Official information can change, so always verify the current rules before travel.

Vietnam's official eVisa system states that eVisas can be valid for up to 90 days and can be issued for single or multiple entry. Applicants apply online, complete payment and receive the result online. The official application also requires passport and personal information, travel details and a portrait photo.

The official portal also states that the eVisa application fee is non-refundable if the application is denied. Entry and exit must be through designated border gates.

FAQ

Common visa questions

Does everyone need a Vietnam eVisa?

Not necessarily. Visa requirements depend on nationality and the applicable exemption or entry rules. Check the current official information or contact Oravia for an assessment.

How long can a Vietnam eVisa be valid?

The official eVisa system currently states a maximum validity of 90 days, with single or multiple entry options.

What information is needed for an eVisa application?

The official application asks for passport and personal information, a portrait photo, travel dates, intended entry/exit information and other trip details. citeturn0search0

Can Oravia guarantee visa approval?

No. Visa decisions are made by the relevant Vietnamese authorities. Oravia is a private assistance service.

Can you help if I made a mistake?

Yes. Our Fix eVisa Service is designed to assess application issues and explain the available next step. The exact solution depends on the case.

Can you help at weekends or holidays?

We offer a dedicated Visa at Weekends & Holidays service for urgent situations, subject to actual team availability and the processing options available at the time.

Important: Oravia is a private visa-assistance service and is not the Vietnamese government. Visa eligibility, approval and processing are determined by the competent Vietnamese authorities. Always verify current requirements through the official source.
