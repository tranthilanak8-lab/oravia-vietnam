Noi Bai Airport Fast Track (HAN), Hanoi

Arrive in Hanoi the easy way: meet your assistant and move through immigration faster.

Noi Bai International Airport (HAN) is the main international gateway to Hanoi and northern Vietnam. International flights use Terminal 2, while most domestic flights use Terminal 1. When several international flights arrive together, the immigration hall can get very busy.

With Oravia's Noi Bai Fast Track, your assistant meets you after you get off the plane and guides you through arrival formalities as quickly as possible.

What's included at Noi Bai

  • Meet & greet in the arrival area of Terminal 2, with your name on a sign
  • Escort to the priority immigration lane, where available
  • Assistance with arrival procedures
  • Guidance to baggage claim and customs
  • Escort to the arrival hall to meet your driver

After Fast Track: getting to Hanoi

Noi Bai is about 30 km from Hanoi's Old Quarter. Depending on traffic, the drive usually takes 45 to 60 minutes. Book a private airport transfer and your driver will be waiting when you walk out of the arrival hall.

Heading straight to Ha Long Bay, Ninh Binh or Sapa? We can arrange a private car with driver directly from the airport.

Connecting to a domestic flight in Hanoi?

If you arrive on an international flight at Terminal 2 and continue on a domestic flight, you may need to change terminals. Our Domestic Fast Track can guide you through the connection.

What to send us

  • Flight number and arrival date and time
  • Number of passengers
  • Your visa status: eVisa, visa exemption or other
  • Whether you also need a transfer
