Cu Chi & Mekong Delta Eco Day Trip

Full day with lunch · small group (max 12)

History and countryside in one day: the Cu Chi Tunnels in the morning, then a tuk tuk ride through coconut groves, a rowboat on a small canal and a farm lunch by the river in Ben Luc.

DurationFull day (about 10 hours)
RouteHo Chi Minh City · Cu Chi · Ben Luc
MealsLunch
Pick-upDistrict 1 hotels or a central meeting point

Itinerary

  1. 07:30

    Pick-up in District 1.

  2. 09:00

    Explore the Cu Chi Tunnels and learn how people lived and fought underground.

  3. 11:00

    Drive to the Mekong Delta (about 1.5 hours).

  4. 12:30

    Lunch with river views in Ben Luc.

  5. 13:30

    Tuk tuk through villages and coconut groves, visit a guava orchard and a coconut farm, row along a small canal, try traditional water scooping and relax with a fresh coconut in a hammock.

  6. 16:00

    Drive back, arriving around 17:30–18:00.

Included

  • Air-conditioned transfer
  • English-speaking guide
  • Private boat and sampan
  • Farm lunch
  • Entrance fees and bottled water

Not included

  • Travel insurance
  • Drinks and meals not listed
  • Tips and personal expenses

Good to know

  • Children under 3 travel free (one child per two adults); ages 3–9 pay 75% of the adult price; ages 10 and over pay the adult price.
  • Timings are approximate and may change with traffic and weather.
