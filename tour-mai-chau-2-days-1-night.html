Multi-day Tours · North Vietnam · Mai Chau – Pu Luong

Mai Chau 2 Days / 1 Night – Valley Adventure

Cycling, trekking and a night in a Thai stilt house

Slow down in Mai Chau Valley: cycle between rice fields and Thai villages, spend the night in a stilt-house homestay with a traditional dinner, then trek to the remote villages of Na Meo, Na Mo and Xam Pa.

Duration2 days / 1 night
RouteHanoi · Mai Chau · Na Meo · Xam Pa
Meals2 lunches · 1 dinner · 1 breakfast
Pick-upHanoi Old Quarter

Itinerary

Day 1 · Hanoi – Mai Chau Valley (L/D)

  1. 07:00 – 07:30

    Pick-up at your hotel in Hanoi's Old Quarter. If the street cannot be reached because of traffic, we will meet you at a convenient nearby point.

  2. 07:30 – 10:45

    Drive west along winding mountain roads, through the countryside of the Muong and Thai people, where villages sit between valleys and limestone hills.

  3. 10:50 – 11:30

    Stop at the top of Thung Khe Pass (weather permitting) for a panoramic view over Mai Chau Valley, and browse the small roadside market selling Muong produce.

  4. 12:00

    Lunch in Mai Chau (vegetarian options available).

  5. 14:30

    Cycle with your guide past rice fields and peaceful Thai villages, watching farmers at work and local women weaving scarves and brocade.

  6. 17:00

    Free time to cycle, walk or shop for handicrafts.

  7. 19:00

    Dinner of traditional Thai dishes. Groups of more than 5 can enjoy a Thai dance show and taste "ruou can", rice wine drunk through bamboo straws. Overnight at a stilt-house homestay.

Day 2 · Mai Chau – Remote villages – Hanoi (B/L)

  1. 07:30

    Breakfast at the homestay.

  2. 08:30

    With a local Thai guide, cycle 2 km then trek to the remote villages of Na Meo, Na Mo and Xam Pa, with rice terraces and a closer look at local life and customs.

  3. 12:00

    Back to the homestay to check out, then lunch.

  4. 13:00

    Free time to relax or shop.

  5. 15:30

    Drive back to Hanoi.

  6. 19:00

    Arrive in Hanoi and drop-off at your hotel.

Included

  • Return transport from Hanoi in an air-conditioned van or bus
  • 1 night in a stilt-house homestay
  • English-speaking tour guide
  • Meals as listed in the itinerary
  • Entrance fees

Not included

  • Drinks
  • Personal expenses
  • Tips for guide and driver

Good to know

  • Timings are approximate and may change with traffic and weather; all sights in the itinerary are still included.
  • Bring long-sleeved clothes, a hat, walking shoes, sunscreen and insect repellent.
  • Pack light: a small bag is easiest at the homestay.
