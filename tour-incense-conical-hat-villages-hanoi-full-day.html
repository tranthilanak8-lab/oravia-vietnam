Craft Villages & Hanoi Heritage

Incense village · Conical hat village · Temple of Literature · Hoa Lo Prison

Combine a morning in two traditional craft villages with an afternoon at two of Hanoi's most important historic sites.

DurationFull day (about 9 hours)
RouteHanoi · craft villages
MealsLunch
Pick-upHanoi Old Quarter hotels

Itinerary

  1. 07:30

    Pick-up from your hotel in the Old Quarter or the Opera House area, then a 1.5-hour drive to the craft villages.

  2. 09:30

    Quang Phu Cau incense village: see how incense is made by hand and visit a family that dyes the colourful sticks.

  3. 10:15

    Chuong Village, home of the conical hat for over 300 years. Meet the artisans and decorate your own hat.

  4. 12:45

    Lunch: eight Vietnamese dishes at a local restaurant.

  5. 13:30 – 13:45

    The Temple of Literature, Vietnam's first university, founded in the 11th century.

  6. 15:00

    Hoa Lo Prison, the colonial-era prison later known as the “Hanoi Hilton”.

  7. 16:00 – 16:30

    Tour ends. Drop-off at your hotel or Train Street.

Included

  • Shuttle bus
  • Experienced guide
  • Bottled water
  • Entrance tickets
  • Vietnamese lunch
  • A conical hat you decorate yourself

Not included

  • Drinks
  • Tips for guide and driver
  • Personal expenses
