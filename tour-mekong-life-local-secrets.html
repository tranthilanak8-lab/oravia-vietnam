Mekong Life & Local Secrets

Full day with cooking class · small group (max 12)

A quieter side of the Mekong Delta: a country market, a rice mill and rice-wine workshop, a private boat through coconut-lined waterways, an organic fruit garden and a hands-on cooking class with lunch.

DurationFull day (about 9 hours)
RouteHo Chi Minh City · Mekong Delta
MealsLunch (cooking class)
Pick-upDistrict 1 hotels or a central meeting point

Itinerary

  1. 07:30

    Pick-up in District 1.

  2. 08:30

    Wander a lively countryside market.

  3. 09:30

    Visit a rice mill and a sticky-rice wine workshop.

  4. 10:30

    Private boat ride along quiet waterways.

  5. 11:00

    Explore a coconut and fruit garden and taste fresh fruit.

  6. 12:30

    Cook Vietnamese pancakes, spring rolls and grilled pork in betel leaves, then enjoy a home-style lunch.

  7. 14:00

    Sampan ride through a shaded canal.

  8. 15:00

    Drive back, arriving around 16:30–17:00.

Included

  • Air-conditioned transfer
  • English-speaking guide
  • Private boat and sampan
  • Cooking class and lunch
  • Bottled water

Not included

  • Travel insurance
  • Drinks and meals not listed
  • Tips and personal expenses

Good to know

  • Children under 3 travel free (one child per two adults); ages 3–9 pay 75% of the adult price; ages 10 and over pay the adult price.
  • Timings are approximate and may change with traffic and weather.
