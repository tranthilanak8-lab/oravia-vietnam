Cham Island Snorkeling Day Trip

Full day with lunch

Speed boat out to the Cham Islands, about 25 nautical miles off Hoi An, to visit a fishing village and old temples, then snorkel over coral reefs at Chong Beach.

DurationFull day (about 8 hours)
RouteCua Dai · Cham Islands
MealsLunch
Pick-upDa Nang hotels

Itinerary

  1. 07:30

    Pick-up at your hotel and drive to Cua Dai pier.

  2. 09:15

    Speed boat to the Cham Islands.

  3. 10:30

    Visit Au Thuyen boat shelter, the old Hai Tang Pagoda and the Ong Temple.

  4. 11:30

    Snorkel among coral reefs and colourful fish at Chong Beach.

  5. 12:30

    Lunch at a local restaurant, then free time to relax or take photos.

  6. 14:00

    Speed boat back to the mainland.

  7. 15:00 – 15:30

    Drop-off at your hotel. Tour ends.

Included

  • Transport
  • English-speaking tour guide
  • Entrance fees
  • Speed boat and snorkelling equipment
  • Lunch
  • Bottled water

Not included

  • Drinks
  • Tips for guide and driver
  • Personal expenses

Good to know

  • Children under 4 pay a small fee (please ask); ages 4–9 pay 50% of the adult price; ages 10 and over pay the adult price.
  • Sea conditions permitting; trips may be cancelled in rough weather.
  • Pick-up from Hoi An hotels can be arranged on request.
  • The order of the itinerary may change on the day.
