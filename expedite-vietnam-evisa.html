Expedite Your Vietnam eVisa

Already applied and still waiting? Let us look at your application before your flight date gets too close.

You applied for your Vietnam eVisa on time, but the status still says "processing" and your departure date is getting close. This is one of the most stressful moments before a trip, especially if your airline asks to see your eVisa at check-in.

Oravia's Expedite eVisa service is for travellers who have already submitted an application on the official portal and need help moving it forward. We review your case and, where options are available, work to speed up processing so you receive your result in time.

Expedite or Urgent Visa: which one do you need?

  • Expedite eVisa: you have already applied and have an application code, but the result is not ready.
  • Urgent Visa: you have not applied yet and your flight is close.

When to ask us to expedite your eVisa

  • Your flight leaves in the next few days and the status is still "processing"
  • Your application has taken longer than the usual processing time
  • Your plans changed and you now need to travel earlier
  • A public holiday or weekend is coming and you are worried about further delays

How it works

  1. Send us your application code, your nationality, flight date and time, and arrival airport.
  2. We check your application and tell you honestly what options are available for your timeframe.
  3. We confirm the service and quote before you pay.
  4. We follow up and let you know as soon as your eVisa is ready. Then we help you check it against your passport.

Important to know

  • Processing decisions are made by the Vietnamese immigration authorities. We cannot guarantee a result or an exact time.
  • If the information in your application is incorrect, speeding it up will not help. We will tell you if you need to fix your eVisa instead.
  • Please do not submit a second application for the same trip before talking to us. Duplicate applications can cause further delays.

Is your eVisa still processing?

Send us your application code and flight date. We will check your case and explain the options.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a Vietnam eVisa that is already submitted be expedited?

In many cases there are options to move an application forward, depending on its status, your nationality and the timeframe. Send us your application code and we will check what is possible.

Should I apply again if my eVisa is taking too long?

Not without advice. A second application for the same trip can create confusion and further delays. Contact us first.

How fast can you get my eVisa?

It depends on your case and on processing by the authorities. We will tell you what is realistic before you confirm the service.

What do I need to send you?

Your eVisa application code, nationality, the email used to apply, and your flight date, time and arrival airport. Please do not send passport images until we provide a secure way to do so.
