Can I arrange my visa and tour through Oravia?

Yes. Visa assistance and inbound tours are the two core services of Oravia, so travellers can enquire about both in one place.

Are Oravia tours private or group tours?

Both. We can offer private journeys and tailor-made experiences, depending on the itinerary.

Can I customise a tour?

Yes. Tell us your dates, interests, number of travellers and preferred hotel level and we can build a tailored itinerary.

Do I need a visa to enter Vietnam?

It depends on your nationality and current Vietnamese entry rules. Check the current official rules or ask Oravia to assess your situation.
