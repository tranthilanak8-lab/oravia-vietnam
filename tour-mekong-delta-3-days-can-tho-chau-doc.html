Mekong Delta 3 Days: Can Tho & Chau Doc

Option to continue to Phnom Penh · group up to 25

The full delta journey: Cai Be's waterways and cooking class, Cai Rang floating market in Can Tho, the Tra Su flooded forest and Chau Doc, then back to Saigon or on to Phnom Penh by speedboat.

Duration3 days / 2 nights
RouteHo Chi Minh City · Cai Be · Can Tho · Chau Doc
Meals2 breakfasts · 2–3 lunches · 1 dinner
Pick-upDistrict 1 hotels or a central meeting point

Itinerary

Day 1 · Ho Chi Minh City – Cai Be – Can Tho (L)

  1. 07:00 – 07:30

    Pick-up in District 1.

  2. Day

    Boat trip in Cai Be with an old house and coconut candy workshop, fruit and folk music, a sampan ride, lunch with a cooking class and a village bike ride to Ba Kiet's ancient house.

  3. Evening

    Transfer to Can Tho. Overnight in Can Tho.

Day 2 · Cai Rang – Tra Su Forest – Chau Doc (B/L/D)

  1. Morning

    Boat to Cai Rang Floating Market, a noodle workshop, Munir Ansay Khmer Pagoda and the Con Son orchards.

  2. Afternoon

    Lunch in Can Tho, drive to Chau Doc and explore Tra Su flooded forest by rowing boat.

  3. Evening

    Dinner and overnight in Chau Doc.

Day 3 · Option 1: Chau Doc – Phnom Penh (B)

  1. 06:30

    Breakfast and check-out.

  2. 07:00

    Speedboat to Phnom Penh (about 5 hours). Tour ends in Phnom Penh.

Day 3 · Option 2: Chau Doc – Ho Chi Minh City (B/L)

  1. Morning

    A floating village, a Cham village, Chau Doc market, Hang Pagoda and Ba Chua Xu Temple.

  2. Afternoon

    Lunch on the way and drive back, arriving around 17:30–18:00.

Included

  • Coach transfers and boat trips
  • English-speaking guide
  • Entrance fees
  • Meals as listed
  • 1 night in a 3-star hotel in Can Tho and 1 night in Chau Doc (twin/double share)
  • Speedboat or bus ticket to Phnom Penh (option 1)
  • Bottled water daily

Not included

  • Travel insurance
  • Drinks and meals not listed
  • Tips and personal expenses
  • Single room supplement
  • Cambodia visa (option 1)

Good to know

  • Children under 3 travel free (one child per two adults); ages 3–9 pay 75% of the adult price; ages 10 and over pay the adult price.
  • Timings are approximate and may change with traffic and weather.
